# The CRM jobs, step by step in the Wix Editor

The Wix connector can't change what's on the pages of this site (checked
18/10: it reaches the site's data, not the Editor's pages, text, buttons,
menu or footer). So these three jobs are done by hand in the Wix Editor.
Each takes a few minutes. Steps are from Wix's Help Center (links below).

**Open the Editor:**
https://editor.wix.com/html/editor/web/renderer/edit/81450aca-96ab-42f3-a6ad-7576581f7a06?metaSiteId=0220a2a1-ecdc-4b7a-8952-aac619d33eaa

Links don't work inside the Editor: test them in Preview or after
publishing. Nothing goes live until you press **Publish**. After
publishing, check each change on a phone as well as a computer.

---

## 1. Privacy policy link in the footer (open list #2)

Agreed (Sammy, 18/10): the footer links to the CRM's privacy page, which
has a section about the website. Goes under the WhatsApp line in the
footer's Contact us column (see the preview).

1. Click **Add** on the left of the Editor, then **Text**, and drag a
   paragraph into the footer, under the Tel and WhatsApp lines.
2. Click **Edit Text** and type: `Privacy policy`
3. With the text box selected, click the **Link** icon, choose **Web
   Address**, and paste: `https://crm.round1boxfit.co.uk/privacy`
4. Choose **Current window**, then **Done**.
5. Match the font and colour to the footer's other text, and underline
   it so it reads as a link.

Wix's guides: [adding a link to text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-a-link-to-text),
[privacy policy in the footer](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-your-privacy-policy-or-terms-and-conditions-to-your-sites-footer).

## 2. WhatsApp number next to the landline (open list #3)

In the footer (on every page), under `Tel: 0117 2510 120`.

1. Click the text box with the landline, then **Edit Text**.
2. Add a new line under Tel, in the same style: `WhatsApp: 07536 138295`
3. Highlight that line, click the **Link** icon, choose **Web Address**,
   and paste: `https://wa.me/447536138295`
   (this opens a WhatsApp chat with the gym; replies come into the CRM's
   WhatsApp inbox).
4. Choose **New window**, then **Done**.
5. On **Contact us**, under "Like what you see? Get in touch if you have
   any questions", add: `Or WhatsApp us on 07536 138295 · Save our number`.
   Link `07536 138295` to `https://wa.me/447536138295` and `Save our
   number` to `https://crm.round1boxfit.co.uk/contact` (the card that adds
   the gym to their phone's contacts). Underline both.

Wix's guide: [adding a link to text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-a-link-to-text).

## 3. Button to the 28 Day Program form (open list #4)

On **Home** and **Memberships**.

**Wording** (Claude's suggestion, 18/10, in the preview): button
`Start your 28 days`, and under it, where there's room:
`Answer a few quick questions, then book your intro meeting.` (true to
the form: about 12 questions, then they book an in-person intro meeting).

Where (as in the preview):
- **Home, computer:** under "Training for all.", in place of the small
  "Explore" text. The hero is too short for the line underneath; make it
  a little taller in the Editor to add it, or leave the line off here.
- **Home, phone:** in the dark band under the photo, in place of
  "Explore" and its arrow, with the line underneath. (Wix keeps a
  separate phone layout: switch to it at the top of the Editor and
  check the button sits there.)
- **Memberships:** under the Join now button, with the line underneath.

1. Add a button: [Wix's guide to buttons](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-about-buttons)
   (a text button).
2. Click **Change Text** (or the button's settings) and type the wording.
3. Click the **Link** icon, choose **Web Address**, and paste:
   `https://crm.round1boxfit.co.uk/f/free-trial`
   (a plain link: the CRM records the enquiry as coming from the website).
4. Choose **Current window**, then **Done**.
5. Style it like the site's red Join now button: brand red (#EC2024) with
   black text, the same font, uppercase and spaced out, square corners.

Wix's guide: [adding a link to a button](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-a-link-to-a-button).
